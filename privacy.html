Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

CineHelm is a private tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels and his own Google Drive files.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorization, CineHelm uses the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API and Google Drive (only files created by the app) to upload and edit the owner's videos, read the analytics of the owner's channels and store the owner's own working documents.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to run the owner's channels. Access tokens and any data retrieved are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google consent screen.

CineHelm's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
